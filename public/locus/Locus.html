Overview

A portfolio at a glance

Agencies juggle properties, tenants, repairs, leads and money across dozens of spreadsheets and inboxes. Locus pulls all of it into one calm, data-dense workspace.

The web console opens on a live map of the portfolio, monthly performance, listed properties and cashflow: then drills into full listing records, a properties grid and tiered subscription plans. The companion app keeps the field team in sync: bookings, offers, maintenance requests and filtered search.

We owned the experience and the front-end across both platforms: the dashboard, listing and properties views, the onboarding and plans, the mobile flows, and the underlying design system of forms, tables, dialogs and charts.
