Overview

A bank young people actually open

Most banking apps talk to adults who already understand banking. Al Hilal needed to speak to a younger audience (and the parents guiding them) without losing the trust a bank has to earn.

I designed the experience around a single idea: turn pocket money into life lessons. The onboarding tells that story in three confident screens, and the home turns the account into a rewards-driven lifestyle hub rather than a list of transactions.

I owned the flow, the UI against the bold AHB visual language (midnight indigo, Hilal magenta, sunrise orange) and built the front-end so the shipped app matched the design exactly.
