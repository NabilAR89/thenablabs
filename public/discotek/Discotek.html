Overview

Sell the room, in the dark

A club lives or dies on its line-up and its energy, and a website has to bottle both before anyone's through the door. Discotek needed to feel like the night, not describe it.

I built a full-bleed, dark experience where photography does the talking: a disco-ball hero, an events carousel headlining the next big act, a resident roster, a charged photo gallery and a frictionless "Book a table" path.

I owned the experience and the front-end: the immersive hero, the events slider, the gallery grid, the contact flow and a glowing cyan-magenta visual system that holds up over busy imagery.
